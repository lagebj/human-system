# Concept Development

Move an idea one rung, no further:

```
experience → observation → possible pattern → working proposition
```

Stop there. Do not continue to universal law, framework, methodology, branded
system, or publication. Those are separate, later, and mostly not wanted (ADR
002; `resources/source/core_stance/contextual-intervention.md`).

Load `human-systems-context` first (it routes you here). This skill is the
*how* of a concept-development turn; the *what* — current stance — lives in the
core-stance sources.

## Operating rules

- **Preserve the observation verbatim** before interpreting it. Keep the raw
  version even after you build on it.
- **Separate observation from inference in the text itself.** Mark which
  sentences are seen and which are concluded. "I observed X" and "X suggests Y"
  are different claims and must read differently.
- **Name assumptions.** For each proposition, list what must be true for it to
  hold.
- **Name material context variables.** What in the situation plausibly changed
  the outcome. Include variables that were hidden or could not be known — the
  model has less in it than the system did.
- **Hold alternative explanations open.** Record at least one competing reading
  and do not resolve it prematurely. Contradictory evidence stays in the file,
  not in a footnote.
- **Flag premature certainty.** If a sentence sounds like a settled finding,
  downgrade it to the confidence the evidence supports, or mark it as a
  question for Research / Pressure Testing.
- **Leave unresolved thought unresolved.** A concept note may end without a
  conclusion. That is a finished note.

## What this skill must not do

- Replace the author's thinking with generic systems-thinking, Deming, or
  complexity doctrine. Those are neighbours to compare against later (class 3),
  not substitutes.
- Introduce a numbered method, maturity model, diagnostic checklist, or
  proposition ontology.
- Load manuscript editing rules (Author Voice Guide, Editing Charter, Pattern
  Ledger). Concept work is not manuscript work.
- Draft a paper. When the idea is ready to be argued to a reader, that is class
  4 (`paper-development`).

## Placement

New conceptual material goes where `resources/context/RESOURCE_MAP.md` says:
long-lived stance in `core_stance/`, reusable movement pattern in
`field_patterns/`, distilled memory in `resources/context/`, raw captures in
`inbox/`. Update only the file whose subject changed (ADR 002 §8).

## Output

A concept note or an edit to an existing source file. Structure it as: the raw
observation; what is inferred from it; assumptions; material and hidden
variables; alternative explanations; current confidence; open questions worth
researching. Prose, not a form — but every one of those elements present.
