## Purpose

This profile describes author language behaviour specific to **reflective / conceptual** surfaces (Human System papers, essays, conceptual notes). Use this in addition to the base `LANGUAGE_MODEL.md` and `THOUGHT_MOVEMENT_MODEL.md`.

---

## Strongly supported observations (this surface)

### 1. Long accumulative sentences
**Observation:** Sentences accumulate clauses and factors without breaking. 50-80+ words is common.

**Evidence:**
- `hs-2026-09-01-it-depends`: 80+ word sentence listing factors
- `hs-2026-09-01-human-system`: 40+ word definition

**Confidence:** high

### 2. First-person epistemic stance
**Observation:** "I think", "I love it", "I am not sure", "I have noticed" dominate. Claims are owned.

**Evidence:**
- `hs-2026-09-01-it-depends`: "I actually love it"
- `hs-2026-09-01-uncertainty`: "I am not sure about these things myself"
- `hs-2026-09-01-background`: "I think there are some important perspectives"

**Confidence:** high

### 3. Uncertainty increases through the piece
**Observation:** Starts with a position, becomes less certain as variables are added.

**Evidence:**
- `hs-authorship-evolution`: moves from "I have a problem" to "I do not always have the answers"
- `hs-it-depends-human-system`: starts with "I actually love it", ends with compression after expanding variables

**Confidence:** high

### 4. Dry humour via absurd escalation
**Observation:** Lists reasonable factors, then escalates to absurd ones for contrast.

**Evidence:**
- `hs-2026-09-01-it-depends`: "which color it has, what shape it has, what country of origin it has"

**Confidence:** moderate

### 5. Opposite comparison for emphasis
**Observation:** "Everyone hates... but I actually love it" structure used to expose contradiction.

**Evidence:**
- `hs-2026-09-01-it-depends`: "Everyone hates when a consultant says this, but I actually love it"

**Confidence:** high

### 6. Compression after exploration
**Observation:** After long exploration, compresses into very short statement (3-8 words).

**Evidence:**
- `hs-2026-09-01-it-depends`: "Because it depends."
- `hs-2026-09-01-infra`: "People and their feelings and reactions are also infra"

**Confidence:** high

### 7. Unresolved endings
**Observation:** Does not force closure. "I do not know yet" is valid.

**Evidence:**
- `hs-2026-09-01-uncertainty`: "even though I do not always have the answers"
- `hs-authorship-evolution`: ends with request for depth, not resolution

**Confidence:** high

### 8. Informal/formal gap awareness
**Observation:** Notices difference between formal structure and informal reality.

**Evidence:**
- `hs-2026-09-01-human-system`: "official and unofficial hierarchies, decisions, official and unofficial decision makers"

**Confidence:** high

## Generation guidance (this surface)

### Do
- Allow sentences to accumulate when exploring variables
- Use first-person for claims and uncertainty
- Let uncertainty increase or remain stable (do not force resolution)
- Use absurd escalation sparingly for dry humour
- Compress after exploration (earn the short statement)
- Allow unresolved endings
- Notice informal/formal gaps

### Do not
- Break long sentences merely for readability
- Front-load all hedging (state position, then qualify)
- Insert humour per quota
- Force closure when thinking is unresolved
- Use abstract vocabulary when ordinary words work
- Hide behind institutional neutrality
