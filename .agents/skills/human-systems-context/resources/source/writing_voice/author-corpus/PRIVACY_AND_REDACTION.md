# Privacy and Redaction

The author corpus may eventually live in a public repository.

Language evidence must not override confidentiality.

## Before adding a sample

Check for:

- client/customer names;
- internal project names that are not public;
- private people;
- email addresses;
- phone numbers;
- credentials;
- internal URLs;
- infrastructure identifiers;
- confidential business facts;
- sensitive personal material.

## Redaction principle

Preserve syntax where practical.

Example:

`I asked [redacted:vendor] whether this is actually supported, because technically possible is not the same thing as maintainable.`

Do not paraphrase the whole sentence merely to hide one noun.

Record that a redaction happened.

## Exclusions

Some samples are better excluded entirely when redaction would remove the very movement that makes the sample useful.

Do not commit private material just because it is stylistically valuable.
