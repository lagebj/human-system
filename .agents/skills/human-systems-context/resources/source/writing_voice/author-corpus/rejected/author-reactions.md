# Author reactions to generated language

These are direct author reactions available in the seed history. They are negative evidence, not universal rules.

## Too clean

> the language is too "clean", it should be more uncertain, more "messy" (thinking-like patterns), less perfect and slightly more emotional, but also slightly more humorous (not comedy ofcourse)

## Too processed

> No, the new re-written text still feels to "AI" and "processed" and "polished"

## Core public-language problem

> I have a problem with the way the agent is writing, how it builds sentences, how it uses lists/bullets etc.

## Desired outcome

> The ultimate goal is to land a language, way of writing and feeling that reflects what I think without it turning into AI mush

These statements should guide comparison against generated drafts. Do not turn their individual words into banned vocabulary or numeric style targets.
