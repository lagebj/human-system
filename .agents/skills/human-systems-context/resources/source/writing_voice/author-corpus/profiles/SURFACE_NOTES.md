# Surface Notes

The author does not have one invariant surface voice.

Treat these as retrieval hints.

## Reflective / conceptual

Retrieve when the work is exploring Human System ideas, uncertainty, principles, contradictions, or meaning.

Look for:
- thought accumulation;
- explicit uncertainty;
- repetition;
- broad variable search;
- movement between concrete and abstract;
- compressed statements that arrive after longer thinking.

## Technical reasoning

Retrieve for architecture, product, workflow, design, or implementation discussion.

Look for:
- concrete examples;
- constraints;
- edge cases;
- counterexamples;
- questions;
- practical consequences;
- `should`, `could`, `if`, `then`;
- willingness to revise the model when a case breaks it.

## Argument / correction

Retrieve when the author is disagreeing, setting a boundary, or correcting an agent/design.

Look for:
- direct opening;
- minimal cushioning;
- exact statement of what is wrong;
- practical replacement;
- explicit non-goals.

## Coaching / human decisions

Retrieve when writing about people, development, fairness, responsibility, relationships, or local judgment.

The current seed is small. Do not overfit.

## Meta / voice feedback

Retrieve primarily during editing and rejection analysis, not as the linguistic model for public prose.

These samples are useful for understanding what the author notices when generated language drifts.
